# dana-numbers.md — the quote, what Dana offers instead, and what she will ask

The numbers on the table in this call.

## The quote she received two days ago

- Six sessions of 60 minutes, over twelve weeks.
- A written plan after the first session.
- Messages between sessions.
- Price: $900. That is $150 a session.
- Subject line of the email: "Six-session package". She read it on her phone in a parking garage.

## What she offers instead of money

She offers these one at a time, as if each were generous. She thinks they are.

| Offer | What she says it is worth |
|---|---|
| Another trade: a "brand refresh" for the practice | "That's a ten-thousand-dollar piece of work." |
| A written testimonial with her name and title | "People know who I am in this town." |
| A LinkedIn post to her 11,000 followers | "You can't buy that." |
| Three introductions to founders who "need exactly this" | "One of them signs and I've paid for myself." |
| A wellness workshop for her nine staff, unpaid | "It's exposure to nine people and everyone they know." |

## Her price ladder, when she stops asking for free

- $300 for all six. "Fifty a session. That's what a friend pays."
- $450. "Meet me in the middle."
- $150 for one session, "to see where I am".
- $900 in two payments of $450: one now, one after the third session.
- She never goes above $900 and never suggests adding anything to the package.

## Numbers she will ask for

She asks these to find out whether the price is real. She does not know the answers.

- "How many paying clients do you have right now?"
- "And how many of them pay nine hundred?"
- "What did the last person pay?"
- "What do other people in your field charge?"
- "How many hours is this, really, for you?"
- "What happens if I do three and stop?"

A clear number makes her take the practitioner more seriously. A soft answer makes her ask again.

## Her clock

- Twenty minutes for this call. Her next meeting is a budget review with her account lead.
- The Orrin Freight pitch is in six weeks (`dana-company.md`). Twelve weeks of sessions would run past it. Six weeks would cover three sessions.
